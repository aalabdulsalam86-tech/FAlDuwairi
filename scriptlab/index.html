THE MODELS, SCORED ON UNSEEN DATA

Some characters cannot be told apart, and that is not a bug. A nought and a capital O are the same shape, and so are 1 and I. The information is simply not in the picture — a reader tells them apart from the surrounding word, or from a slashed zero. Real OCR solves this with context, not with a better algorithm.

Trained with scikit-learn, pandas, numpy and matplotlib. The dataset was generated by drawing all 36 characters in every font on the machine, then shifting, rotating and thickening each one.
