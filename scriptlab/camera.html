What it can recognise. The model knows 80 everyday object types. From your list: person, laptop, keyboard, mouse, cup (a mug), tv (a monitor) and dining table all work. A pen does not — it is not one of the 80, so the model has no label for it and will either ignore it or guess something close, usually scissors or toothbrush. That is a good thing to show on purpose: a model can only ever answer with a class it was taught. Other useful ones nearby: cell phone, book, bottle, chair, clock, backpack, scissors.
